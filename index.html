Challenge & role

Increase organic visibility and qualified traffic across English and Spanish content. I connected client priorities with content, SEO, social and off-page delivery, while managing timelines, approvals and reporting.

Approach

  • Benchmarked competitors and identified keyword and content gaps.
  • Built a bilingual content roadmap, including optimization and internal linking.
  • Used structured GPT prompts for research and first drafts, followed by human editing, fact-checking and SEO QA.
  • Coordinated off-page support and content promotion.

Results

Monthly organic visits increased from 884 to approximately 7,500 between mid-2024 and May 2025. Ranking keywords grew from 1,729 to more than 7,000.
